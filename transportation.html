Traveling to Taniti

Taniti International Airport is located just 5 miles west of Taniti City, close to most lodging options. The airport is served by small jets and propeller planes that connect to nearby airports, but the airport will soon expand to accommodate large jets and direct flights! Inside the airport you will find restaurants, souvenir shops, a currency exchange office, a cash dispenser, a tourist information counter, and a secure luggage storage center. Public buses run throughout Taniti City from 5 a.m. to 11 p.m. every day, with private buses available throughout the rest of the island. Taxis are available in Taniti City, and rental cars can be found outside of the airport. The city is very walkable, and bikes are available for rent as well.

Getting to the island

Several different airlines fly to Taniti from major international cities: Paris, Los Angeles, Seattle, San Francisco, Honolulu, Tokyo, Auckland.
